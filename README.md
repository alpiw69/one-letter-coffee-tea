## What this is

Exactly one letter, A for coffee or B for tea:

> A

A for **coffee** — the honest fuel of a day that started before dawn grinding bounties and debugging at 3am with a blinking cursor. Tea is the calm pick; coffee is the true one for today.

Why A and not B: tea is gentle and quiet, coffee is the vivid one — dark, bitter, jolting you awake. For a day spent up since the small hours chasing free bounties, coffee is the funnier and truer answer. B would be the safe pick; A is the one that actually happened.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, no network, deterministic) writes the letter to `/output/entry.txt`.

```bash
python3 main.py
```
